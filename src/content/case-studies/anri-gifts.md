## Where did the time go before AI?

Before AI, every new product passed through several hands, from sourcing to pack images to the catalogue, before a customer could see it. Quotations and replies took up hours of the sales day, all assembled by hand.

## What did Human Horizon implement?

Human Horizon co-founder Jaye Wang worked with the founder and team on a pro bono basis to rebuild this work around Claude:

- **Sourcing on demand and automatically.** The team can start sourcing at any time, and competitor availability starts it too.
- **Automated catalogue work.** Products move from import to finished images to the database without manual handling, and a person reviews each one before it goes live.
- **Custom CRM and database software.** Built with the team to run the new sourcing and catalogue workflows.
- **An AI sales assistant.** It drafts email and WhatsApp replies and builds quotations from the catalogue on each customer request, attached to a draft ready to review.

## How was the team trained?

Jaye trained the founder and team to work with Claude, and they built each workflow together.

## What did the team adopt?

Four AI assistants now handle the groundwork for sourcing, products, design and sales, and the team checks what they produce.

The working day now starts with review. Quotations and replies wait in one batch each morning, and the team checks and sends them together.

## Why did the results stick?

The team built these workflows with Jaye, so they understand them and own them. AI is now part of how Anri Gifts runs every day, across sourcing, sales and design, not a tool one person tries now and then. People still make every call that matters: what goes live, what each client receives and which suppliers to work with.

## Where did the time go before AI?

Every new product took days of work before a customer could see it. The team searched for products, compared suppliers by hand and waited for a designer to redo the pack images. Then someone updated the catalogue database and the collaterals that listed the product. Getting one product from supplier to the live site took 5 days.

Quotations had the same problem. Each one meant looking up products and prices across a custom CRM and catalogue that worked poorly with other tools. Preparing quotations and drafting replies to email and WhatsApp enquiries took half of every working day.

## What did Human Horizon implement?

Human Horizon co-founder Jaye Wang worked with the founder and team on a pro bono basis to rebuild this work around Claude:

- **Sourcing on demand and automatically.** Products are sourced when the team asks, and automatically when competitor availability changes.
- **Automated catalogue work.** Catalogue sourcing, importing, image preparation, database management and supplier management are now automated.
- **Custom CRM and database software.** Built with the team to run the new sourcing and sales workflows.
- **An AI sales assistant.** It drafts email and WhatsApp replies and builds quotations from the catalogue on each customer request, attached to a draft ready to review.

## How was the team trained?

Jaye trained the founder and team to work with Claude, and they built each workflow together.

## What did the team adopt?

Four AI assistants now work alongside the team: for sourcing, products, design and sales. They do the groundwork. A person reviews every output before it reaches the website or a customer.

The day starts differently too. Quotations and replies are ready in one batch each morning. The team reviews them all at once and sends them, in about 5 minutes.

## What changed in numbers?

- **Supplier to live product:** 5 days down to 8 minutes.
- **Quotations and replies:** 4 hours down to 5 minutes a day.
- **Where the team's time goes:** away from data entry and image rework, towards supplier relationships and new clients.

## Why did the results stick?

The team built these workflows with Jaye, so they understand them and own them. AI is now part of how Anri Gifts runs every day, across sourcing, sales and design, not a tool one person tries now and then. People still make every call that matters: what goes live, what each client receives and which suppliers to work with.

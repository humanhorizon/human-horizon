## How is it different from automating one task?

Plain [automation](/insights/glossary/automation) often covers a single step, such as sending a confirmation email. Business process automation links the steps of a process together: an order arrives, the confirmation is drafted, documents are prepared, someone approves, and the customer is updated.

## What does AI add?

Many processes start with messy input, such as an email or a scanned form. AI can read it and draft the output, so the process can start from the way people actually write. A person still checks the results that matter.

## Where should a small business begin?

Not with the whole process. Choose one process step that repeats and costs hours, build that, measure the time before and after, then extend. Our guide to [AI automation: where to start](/insights/ai-automation) walks through how.

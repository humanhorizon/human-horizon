## What are examples of automation?

An invoice that is sent on the first of every month. A form submission that creates a row in a spreadsheet and sends a confirmation email. A reminder that goes out three days before a deadline. In each case the steps are fixed, and the same input always gives the same result.

## What can automation not do?

Because the steps are fixed, plain automation struggles with messy input. An email written in free text, or a document laid out differently each time, usually needs a person to read it first. That is where AI can help, by reading the input and drafting the result, with automation carrying the work onward. See [Artificial intelligence (AI)](/insights/glossary/artificial-intelligence-ai).

## Where should a business start with automation?

With a task that repeats and that you can describe step by step. Our guide on [AI automation: where to start](/insights/ai-automation) shows how to choose one. When a whole sequence of tasks is automated end to end, it is called [business process automation](/insights/glossary/business-process-automation).
